# Thy Recorder Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Thy Recorder is designed with privacy in mind. Your recordings remain under your control.

## Data Collection

Thy Recorder does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

Thy Recorder does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

## Screen and Audio Recording

Thy Recorder may request permission to access:

- Screen and system audio recording
- Microphone input
- User-selected folders

These permissions are used only to provide recording functionality.

Screen content, application audio, and microphone audio are processed locally on your Mac.

Thy Recorder does not upload your recordings to our servers.

## Recording Storage

Recordings are saved locally to a folder selected by you.

Thy Recorder does not operate a cloud storage service for your recordings.

## In-App Purchases

Thy Recorder Pro is available as a one-time in-app purchase.

Purchases and payment information are processed by Apple through the App Store. Thy Recorder does not receive or store your payment card information.

## Accounts

Thy Recorder does not require an account.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Thy Recorder's functionality or privacy practices change.
